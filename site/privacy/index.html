Policy plate / legal

Privacy

Effective 27 August 2026. The public documentation site has no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, accounts, or third-party runtime scripts.

The open-source proxy

The binary runs on infrastructure you control. It contacts the public registries and advisory feed URLs you configure. Cache files and JSONL refusal logs stay on your host. We do not receive them. Package requests may contain operationally sensitive names, so restrict access to the cache and audit log.

Local browser data

The policy demo runs entirely in your browser. If you enter or return with an Operator Pack license, the token and its most recent verification verdict are stored in local storage under keys beginning with sb_license:cooldown-registry-proxy. A service worker may cache the public site shell for offline access. Clear this site’s storage to remove both.

Purchase and verification

Optional purchases and license verification are handled by Sociobot and its merchant-of-record provider, Dodo. License verification sends the license token to api.sociobot.in. Their transaction records are governed by the privacy notice shown at checkout. This site does not receive card details.

Questions

Open a repository issue for general questions. Do not post license tokens, private package names, or production logs.
